## 2. AI outputs that have been corrected

AI output is not used just because the AI said so. The items below were changed, rejected or shown to have no effect after checking, ablation tests or the independent audit.

- C022 (AI_LOG A09): the "reporting curve split by claim cause" designed by the coordinator was found by the executing worker to be numerically identical to the original curve (maximum difference 5e-16, the 2024 motor pure premium stays at 585.10); it changes no prediction and is kept only as a spare component.
- C023 (AI_LOG A10): the "frequency × severity" model split by claim cause scored below the best single model in cross-validation (motor 0.0809 vs 0.0893, buildings 0.1045 vs 0.1094, contents 0.0879 vs 0.0911); rejected, not in the final blend.
- C024 (AI_LOG A11): cross-line / household statistical features and treating branch_id as a category gave zero gain in the ablation test (importance rank 60th or lower) and lowered the 2023 fold score of each model (audit B2); rejected, and later dropped by C026.
- C031 (AI_LOG A18): out-of-fold target encoding of motor make_model added only 0.00028 on the two-fold average, short of the +0.002 threshold, and the 2024 fold went down; rejected; the S09 file was restored by SHA256.
- C032 (AI_LOG A19): re-tuning the tree models on the C026 feature set found no config that meets "average gain of at least 0.002 and no regression on either fold" (motor LightGBM was best at +0.0027, but −0.0008 on the 2024 fold); parameters unchanged, S09 stays the final candidate.
- Independent audit B1–B8 (AI_LOG A13, `docs/AUDIT_2026-10-22.md`), adopted items: B1 (motor make_model overfitting, 0.0832 up to 0.0880 after dropping it), B2 (branch_id treated as a category lowers the score) and B3 (category columns with no signal) were merged and implemented as C026; B8 (tree models stop early on the fold being scored, so the score is too high) was implemented as C027 (inner early stopping). Public leaderboard S06 0.12579 to S07 0.12851 (`docs/SUBMISSIONS.md:13-14`); that change also added CatBoost, so the increase cannot all be credited to the audit.
- Audit B4 (trend double counting) was not adopted after the S08 probe (public leaderboard −0.00031); B5–B7 are only recorded and nothing was changed (AI_LOG A13).
- C042 (AI_LOG A23), C045 and C046 (AI_LOG A25, A26), audit P2-F8 (AI_LOG A24): three experiments were rejected on validation, and one error in a diagnostic script was found by the audit and fixed. The C042 contents GLM market interaction term was worse than the plain GLM on all six folds; the nine-candidate blend formally met the gate (+0.0024), but the coordinator rejected it on the grounds of "only a diversity gain, the group folds are in-sample, and the 21% upward shift in the new business level is unverified"; the C045 new business weighted training (new business weight scaled up about 37 times) was worse in every contents view and for every model, the early-stopping-only change was flat and did not pass the gate, and the motor blend without GLM lost 0.00077 on the original time fold; the C046 re-tuning of the emerging market tree models, as a blend candidate, did not pass the gate on any of the three lines. Audit P2-F8: `scripts/part2_new_business_check.py:73-74` read the uncorrected raw exposure (C001 was not used; 0.45% of the training rows are 1.0139 or 365), so the pure premium and the by-year new business / renewal ratios in section 1 were miscalculated (motor 2023 new business pure premium 753.5, correct value 940.6; motor 2023 ratio 2.587, correct value 2.278); this was fixed by using the exposure from the target table (commit 97cd065); the OOF ratios in section 2 quoted by the report draft are not affected, and there is no effect on any score (`docs/AUDIT_PART2.md:27`).
