Limited-participation competition. Only invited users may participate.

This Competition is Part 1 of a two-part hackathon. Prizes will be awarded after Part 2 closes, based on the overall evaluation (Section 2.4), not on the Part 1 leaderboard alone. You cannot enter or submit from multiple Kaggle accounts.

## 1. Competition-specific terms
1. Title: IAA AI-Assisted Actuarial Hackathon 2026 – Part 1
2. Sponsor: International Actuarial Association, 605 - 75 Albert Street, Ottawa, Ontario K1P 5E7, Canada
3. Website: https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/iaa-ai-assisted-hackathon-2026-part-1
4. Prizes: 1st CAD $500 · 2nd CAD $100 · 3rd CAD $50. Only to members or students of an IAA Full or Associate Member Association. One set of prizes covers both parts.

## 2. Competition-specific rules

**2.1 Team limits**
- a. Maximum Team size is three (3).
- b. Team mergers allowed (leader performs); combined submission count must be ≤ submissions/day × days running, as of the Team Merger Deadline.
- c. Teams must be the same in Part 1 and Part 2. No team changes after the Part 1 Team Merger Deadline.

**2.2 Submission limits**
- a. Maximum ten (10) Submissions per day.
- b. You may select up to one (1) Final Submission for judging. If none selected, the best public-leaderboard submission is used. Your Part 1 Final Submission counts towards your overall result.

**2.3 Timeline** — dates shown on page as placeholders "[DATE, TIME UTC]"; rely on the Overview page countdown.

**2.4 Two-part competition**
- a. Part 1 (developed market) on this site. Part 2 (emerging market) on a separate Kaggle page, opens when Part 1 closes. Same Team in both parts.
- b. Final deliverables (video demo, written report, version-controlled code repository) are submitted at the end of Part 2 and must cover both parts. How to submit will be published on the Part 2 site.
- c. Overall ranking after Part 2:
  - Pricing Performance and Adaptation (40%) = private leaderboard scores of Part 1 and Part 2, equally weighted, combined with the panel's assessment of modelling choices, adaptation, and treatment of data quality and uncertainty.
  - Effective Use of AI (25%), Trust/Validation/Guardrails (20%), Communication/Documentation (15%) assessed by the panel from final deliverables.
- d. AI-assisted review tools may screen deliverables; panel decisions are final.
- e. Part 1 leaderboard position alone does not determine prizes.

**2.5 Competition data**
- a. Use only for the Competition and Kaggle forums. **May process the data with AI tools and services, including hosted LLMs, for the purpose of participating.** Data is synthetic, no real personal information.
- b. Data security: prevent access by people who have not agreed to the Rules; do not redistribute/publish the data (except AI processing as in 2.5.a).

**2.6 Winner licence** — winners license the winning submission and code under an OSI-approved licence that does not limit commercial use; may be asked for a reproducible methodology description (architecture, preprocessing, loss, training details, hyper-parameters) and a code repository with reproduction instructions.

**2.7 External data and tools**
- a/b. External data, models and tools allowed if publicly available / reasonably accessible to all at minimal cost. A small subscription for a widely used tool (e.g. an AI assistant or development environment) is acceptable. Proprietary data/tools others could not reasonably obtain are not.
- c. AutoML tools allowed with appropriate licence.

**2.8 Eligibility** — Competition Entity staff can enter but not win; IAA AI Task Force organisers, data creators and judges cannot win prizes.

**2.9 Winner's obligations** — deliver training + inference code, documentation and environment description able to regenerate the winning submission; grant the licence; sign prize documents.

## Kaggle Foundational Rules — key points
- One Kaggle account per person; one team per person.
- Submissions may not use hand labeling or human prediction of validation/test records.
- No private sharing of code or data outside your team; public sharing only on this competition's Kaggle forum/notebooks (deemed OSI-licensed).
- Open-source code used in the model must be under an OSI-approved licence that does not limit commercial use.
- Winners are determined by the **Private Leaderboard** (private test set). Public Leaderboard uses a representative sample of test data. Which rows are public vs private is not disclosed.
- Ties: earliest-entered submission wins.
- You are responsible for time zones and checking deadline changes on the website.
