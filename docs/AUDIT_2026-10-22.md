## 1. Bugs / mismatches (ranked by expected impact on the metric)

**B1. HIGH: motor tree models overfit the 300-level `make_model`.**
- Where: features.py (all string columns become categories) + native categorical splits in LGBM/XGB.
- Evidence (a13, tuned LightGBM, v1 features, tune_cv scoring):

| fold | baseline | without make_model | categorical regularisation (cat_smooth 100, min_data_per_group 2000, cat_l2 50) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2023 | 0.08082 | **0.08868** | 0.07804 |
| 2024 | 0.08492 | **0.08682** | 0.08586 |

- Mean d2_cal 0.0832 → 0.0880, slightly above motor GLM/GAM CV (~0.0883). This explains "linear beats trees on motor" (C021). Baseline reproduces the archived v1 OOF exactly (0.08082).
- make_model takes 32% of LightGBM gain on motor but its frequency dispersion is only 1.66 (a17): weak signal.
- Fix: drop make_model for tree models, or replace it with an out-of-fold target encoding / GLM relativity as one numeric column. XGB (max_cat_to_onehot=1, partition splits) very likely has the same problem (not tested).
- Motor is ~44% of the combined deviance, so this is the largest single improvement.

**B2. HIGH (if v2 were submitted): C024(d) `branch_id` as category hurts.**

- Evidence (a12, buildings, tuned LGBM):

**B3. MEDIUM-HIGH (magnitude unverified): several pure-noise categorical columns take large gain.**
- Frequency dispersion (~1 = no signal, a17): previous_insurer motor 1.08 / buildings 0.97 / contents 0.90; email_domain 0.69 / 1.43 / 1.38; branch_id see B2; buildings document_preference 0.34, occupation 0.67, marital_status 0.13.
- Yet in a6 (tuned LGBM, all years) previous_insurer gets 13% of gain (contents, buildings) and branch_id 5%.

**B4. MEDIUM-LOW: C009 trend double counts the year level already carried by features.**
- Where: train_validate.py line_trend, evaluate.py trend_factor, TREND_SHARE.
- No year feature, but written_premium (+3-6%/yr, a3/a11) and sum_insured (+2-3%/yr) already carry the level; line_trend is fitted on raw developed pure premium. a6 (tuned LGBM, all years):

| line | model's own 2025 / mean-year level | in-sample A/E slope | residual trend needed | currently applied (half trend) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| motor | 1.023 | +0.7%/yr | 1.017 | 1.0175 |
| buildings | 1.107 | +1.9%/yr (2024 A/E 1.045) | 1.048 | 1.095 |
| contents | 1.100 | +0.2%/yr | 1.004 | 1.064 |

- Without written_premium the residual slopes rise to +1.6 / +3.5 / +1.9%/yr, confirming the level enters through premium.
- Out-of-sample support: train 2021-23, predict 2024, no trend: pred/actual motor 1.011, buildings 0.933, contents 0.976.
- Cost of a level error (a7, OOF): ±5% level ≈ 0.0005-0.001 single-line D², ≈ 0.0002-0.0004 combined.
- The public board prefers half trend over none by 0.0009, so 2025 may have a genuine level jump; the global 0.5 share may hide the double counting but the per-line allocation may be off (contents ~6% high, buildings ~2-4% high, motor about right). Fix: per-line residual A/E trend.

**B5. LOW: `load_test` includes under-reported 2024 large losses.** Motor load_test 1.0968 vs load_val (2021-23) 1.1125; large losses are mostly bodily injury (14% reported at 30 days). Motor test level ~1.4% low. Buildings 1.0438 vs 1.0421, contents 1.0216 vs 1.0195: negligible.

**B6. LOW: recoveries only appear on closed claims.** All 14,776 recoveries are on closed claims; open claims have 0 (a1/a2). Closed motor own_damage recovery rate 23.6%, stable by accident year. AY2024 open own_damage incurred 5.45m will see ~1.29m future recoveries: motor AY2024 reported net cost overstated ~1.4%, feeding the trend and 2024 target. Theft minor (4%).

**B7. LOW: v2 history only half fixed.** Cost uses claim_cost_ult but claim_count stays reported. 2024 history count completeness motor 0.928 / buildings 0.950 / contents 0.967 vs ~100% for the history that training rows see. Also row-wise 1/completeness only inflates rows that have claims; IBNR rows stay 0, so test prior_cost on claimed rows is stretched. Fix: build training history "as at inception" (claims with report_date before the row's start only), matching the test information set.

**B8. LOW-MEDIUM (not quantified): tree models early-stop on the scored fold.** tune_cv.py, oof_predictions.py, train_validate.py: trees see the validation labels, GLM/GAM do not; biases OOF comparisons (C018 threshold, C021 blend weights) toward trees. Fix: inner early-stopping split inside the fit years, or iterations taken from the other fold.

## 3. Evidence checks

### A. How much does written_premium alone explain (2024 rows, level-calibrated, reported-cost basis)
written_premium is annualised and independent of row exposure (a3), so the sensible predictor is wp × exposure × completeness × k.

| line | premium-only D² (mean baseline) | premium-only D² (constant-rate baseline) | exposure-only (mean baseline) | model OOF 2024 D² (mean baseline, level-calibrated) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| motor | 0.0558 | 0.0249 | 0.0317 | GLM 0.0848 / GAM 0.0887 / LGBM 0.0849 / XGB 0.0862 |
| buildings | 0.0881 | 0.0472 | 0.0430 | 0.0975 / 0.1105 / 0.1118 / 0.1115 |
| contents | 0.0522 | 0.0205 | 0.0323 | 0.0839 / 0.0905 / 0.0909 / 0.0927 |

Without exposure (wp × comp) is worse: 0.028 / 0.053 / 0.025. Geometric blends of premium with XGB (premium weight 0.1/0.2/0.3) all lower D² (buildings 0.1115 → 0.1112 / 0.1105 / 0.1094; contents 0.0927 → 0.0922 / 0.0909 / 0.0888). Conclusion: premium is far from a sufficient statistic (about half the model D²) and adds nothing beyond the models.

## 4. Metric baseline check
D² = 1 − dev(model)/dev(baseline). Under any constant baseline dev(baseline) is the same for every submission, so the ranking of two submissions does not depend on the baseline definition (algebraic). Verified on 2024 data (a3):

| line | dev, baseline = mean(y) | dev, baseline = constant rate × exposure × comp | model ranking under both |
|---|---|---|---|
| motor | 113.95 | 110.33 | same |
| buildings | 131.46 | 125.82 | same (LGBM 0.1118→0.0719, XGB 0.1115→0.0716) |
| contents | 66.30 | 64.16 | same (XGB 0.0927→0.0625) |

Conversion: D²_rate = 1 − (1 − D²_mean) × dev_mean / dev_rate; the constant-rate D² is ~0.03-0.04 lower per line. The only case that would change rankings is if Kaggle computed D² per line and averaged, which the text does not state and could not be verified.

## 5. Checked, no defect found
- Claim matching: consecutive rows of a policy are contiguous (gap exactly 1 day, no overlap), no rows cross a year. Motor has 338 / 350 claims exactly on a start / end date, each matched once; reconciliation difference 0. Unmatched claims all precede the policy's first row (2020, or 4-499 days before the first 2021-23 row): no off-by-one. policy_id prefix agrees with line 100%; no losses on 2024-12-31.
- Repudiated claims already have zero amounts; recoveries ≥ 0 and never exceed incurred; no negative net cost.
- Weather: every training and test row matches its start-year weather; no gaps.
- Completeness is applied consistently between the fitted target, validation scoring and OOF; no factor duplicated or missing in the validation vs test arithmetic.
- blend.py FILE_TREND_SHARE matches the logs: GLM/GAM full trend (e.g. motor 1.0354), trees 1.0175.
- Categorical levels are built on train + test together.
- Q10 drift is internally consistent: renewal_count +1.0 per year per policy, vehicle_age ~+0.99.
- Leakage re-check: smoke_alarms, high_value_items, lodgers, works_from_home, telematics_score show nothing unusual between claimed and unclaimed rows.
